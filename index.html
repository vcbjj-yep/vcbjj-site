Frequently Asked Questions

Am I too old to start?
No. Most students are in their 40s, 50s and 60s, and most started with zero BJJ experience. No prior martial arts background is required or assumed — every concept is introduced from scratch.
Will I get hurt?
Not if the room is run properly. The pace is set by the people in it — not by the youngest or most aggressive person on the mat. You will be physically challenged, but you won’t be thrown in over your head.
Do I need to be fit first?
No. BJJ builds fitness; it doesn’t require it as an entry condition. Most people start with limited flexibility and cardio, and you will be tired after your first few classes. That passes.
Will I look foolish as a beginner?
You’ll tap (submit) often when you’re new. So does everyone — tapping is how you learn safely. The discomfort of not knowing what to do usually passes within a few sessions.
Can I try a class first?
VCBJJ doesn’t offer trial classes. It’s a deliberate policy that keeps the room consistent for the students already on the mat. If you’d like a lower-pressure start, a private session lets you learn at your own pace. Read the full info page.
What do I wear?
For Monday NoGi: a rash guard and shorts. For Thursday Gi: a BJJ gi. A mouthguard isn’t required for your first session, but get one before you start live rolling. See what to bring.
Are there contracts or sign-up fees?
No contracts, no registration fees, no admin fees and no grading fees — ever. Group classes are RM 65 per session, pay as you go.
Can I just drop in?
Sessions are booked in advance through the member portal. Group classes are capped at 10 students, and private training is by appointment, Monday to Saturday.
Do women train here?
Yes. Women train at VCBJJ from their 20s to their 60s, in the same small-group classes. Read more for women.
